I am a Research Associate at the **University of Pennsylvania**, working with Prof. Yong Chen. I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2022, advised by Prof. Yu Chen and Prof. Yaohua Wu.

My research focuses on **high-dimensional statistical learning, transfer learning, distributed inference, and modern AI**. I develop statistically principled and computationally scalable methods to identify and integrate information most relevant to a target population or scientific objective.
