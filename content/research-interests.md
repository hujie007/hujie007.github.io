- **Adaptive transfer learning:** Identifying and selectively sharing useful information across heterogeneous populations at the model, feature, and subpopulation levels.

- **Federated and distributed learning:** Developing communication-efficient methods for estimation, inference, and adaptation of pretrained models without sharing individual-level data.

- **Efficient and reliable statistical learning:** Designing scalable methods for massive and high-dimensional data, with a focus on networks, latent structures, and causal inference.
